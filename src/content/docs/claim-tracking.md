## Overview

FleetClaim AI brings the evidence, decisions, and collaboration around a vehicle claim into one workspace. This guide explains the key concepts and the steps teams can use to build a consistent process. Start with a small pilot, define who owns each handoff, and expand once your team has a measurable baseline.

## Recommended workflow

Begin by creating a vehicle and recording the claim details, including the unit identifier, incident date, location, and operating context. Upload clear photos from multiple angles and add notes for anything that may not be visible in an image. Assign the claim to the appropriate reviewer, then use the activity history to keep every decision and request in context.

Review AI-assisted observations and estimates before sharing them externally. The platform is designed to accelerate expert work, not remove professional judgment. Correcting an observation or adding a supplement keeps the record accurate and gives teammates a clear explanation of what changed.

## Best practices

- Use consistent naming for vehicles, locations, and claim types.
- Capture required evidence before requesting an approval.
- Keep internal notes separate from customer-facing updates.
- Check status filters daily for blocked or aging claims.
- Export reports regularly during a pilot so improvements are visible.

## Need help?

If a workflow does not match your operation, document the desired handoff and the fields your team needs. A clear example helps administrators configure roles, templates, and integrations. For account questions, contact the FleetClaim AI team through the demo request form.
